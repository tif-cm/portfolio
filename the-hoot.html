I designed print spreads and social storytelling for UH–West Oʻahu's newspaper.

Publication The Hoot (UH–West Oʻahu)
Role Layout Editor & Reporter
Tools Adobe InDesign, Photoshop
Formats Print, Digital Edition & Instagram

As Layout Editor for The Hoot, I design multi-column spreads, typography hierarchies, and print-ready editions for campus journalism. For my reported feature on UH–West Oʻahu's 2019–2025 Online and Distance Learning Tactical Plan, I interviewed university leadership alongside neighbor-island and non-traditional students, then adapted the data and reporting into a 5-slide Instagram carousel to supplement the print spread.
