I designed a local produce exchange for Hawaiʻi communities.

Project Mahiʻai Marketplace
Role UI/UX & Brand Designer
Tools Figma
Deliverables Brand Identity, High-Fidelity UI & UX Copy

Mahiʻai Marketplace is a peer-to-peer produce exchange concept connecting backyard gardeners with neighbors across Hawaiʻi. I created the visual identity, information architecture, and high-fidelity UI screens—focusing on warm, approachable UX copy, clear two-sided onboarding, and frictionless neighborhood pickup flows.
