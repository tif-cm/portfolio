Client Powerful Prep (Test Prep & College Admissions)
Target User High school students and families preparing for standardized tests and college admissions
Surfaces Web, mobile, and app
My Role Content strategy, production, and digital marketing lead

Context & Goal

Powerful Prep is a leader in test preparation and college admissions consulting, recognized by students and educators for consistently delivering strong score gains and admissions outcomes.

I partnered with Powerful Prep to strengthen their digital presence, modernize their educational content library, and expand organic reach across web and email channels.

Problem Statement

Powerful Prep consistently delivered some of the strongest test score gains in the industry, but their digital footprint did not reflect that educational authority. Initial audits revealed that existing site content was underperforming in search rankings and struggling to hold readers' attention.

The goal was to identify content gaps through competitive analysis and educator interviews, then construct a data-driven strategy that would transform a fragmented web presence into an accessible, engaging educational resource.

Process

Content Audit

I kicked off the project with a full audit of existing site content (2,000+ pages) to establish a baseline and prioritize high-impact improvements. Through this process, I built a roadmap to better serve students and families while improving search visibility by:

  • Evaluating the educational value and clarity of each page
  • Assessing user experience, readability, and engagement levels
  • Checking alignment with student and parent search intent
  • Auditing technical SEO performance and content gaps

Content Strategy Development

To develop a baseline of content ideas, I began with in-depth keyword research and competitor analysis.

For additional content ideas, I collaborated with the tutors who work directly with customers and know their needs best.

Using those insights, I developed a comprehensive content strategy to update and rewrite existing content, and produce fresh, keyword-targeted articles designed to bolster the client’s online presence.

To ensure seamless execution of content production, I established and maintained a quarterly content and social media calendar that included:

  • Long- and short-form articles
  • Infographics
  • Email newsletters
  • Social media posts
  • Digital product updates

Collaborative Content Creation

Working from the content calendar, I produced creative briefs and assigned them to our team of researchers and writers. I also drafted, edited, and wrote additional content as needed.

Our joint efforts resulted in a suite of precisely targeted content with educational depth and effective SEO.

This content resonated with our audience and ranked well in search engines.

Launch & Marketing

As we launched this new and refreshed content, I actively analyzed and interpreted SEO insights using tools including Google Analytics, SEMRush, and Ahrefs.

This data served as a compass for making informed improvements to the content strategy by enabling me to pinpoint high-performing keywords, track user behavior, and identify content gaps.
