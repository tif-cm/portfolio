About

I’m a curious storyteller with a deep appreciation for travel, culture, and spending time in nature.

My professional background is anything but traditional. I previously ran my own business as a professional horse trainer, developing equine and human athletes for international-level competition. That demanding industry instilled the intense self-motivation and organizational discipline I rely on today.

In 2009, I pivoted to follow my love for the arts, diving headfirst into documentary photography and journalism as a columnist for Axis Magazine. There, I honed the editorial and visual skills that form the foundation of my work today.

Now, I translate that passion for narrative across the broader creative media landscape, merging storytelling with thoughtful design to build engaging, accessible experiences in both digital and physical spaces.
